# Third-party installers

AtlasFastStart stores original publisher installers locally. It does not modify their contents or provide accounts, activation keys, or product licences.

The public binary mirror is limited to the following installers. All other catalogue entries use publisher download links and are not uploaded as binary assets.

| Release asset | Redistribution basis | Included notice |
|---|---|---|
| 7zip.7zip-26.03.msi | LGPL 2.1, BSD portions and unRAR restriction | `ThirdPartyLicenses/7-Zip.txt`, `ThirdPartyLicenses/LGPL-2.1.txt`; original `7z2603-src.7z` accompanies the installer in the same release |
| Python.Python.3.12-3.12.10.exe | PSF License 2 and licences preserved in the original distribution | `ThirdPartyLicenses/Python-3.12.txt` |
| Python.Python.3.14-3.14.7.exe | PSF License 2 and licences preserved in the original distribution | `ThirdPartyLicenses/Python-3.14.txt` |
| OpenJS.NodeJS.22-22.23.2.msi | MIT and included third-party notices | `ThirdPartyLicenses/Node.js-22.txt` |
| Rustlang.Rustup-1.29.1.exe | MIT or Apache 2.0 | `ThirdPartyLicenses/Rustup-MIT.txt`, `ThirdPartyLicenses/Rustup-Apache-2.0.txt` |

The binary installers are byte-for-byte publisher files. Embedded licence notices are retained. Publisher names and trademarks identify their software and do not imply endorsement.
